How it works

Your time, your choice

01

Choose your apps

Pick the apps you want Kharu to notice. Other apps stay out of your check-ins.

02

Kharu notices

When a visit runs longer than your usual, Kharu asks if staying is still worth it. “Why this?” shows the numbers.

03

Stop on purpose

Choose “I’m done,” take five more minutes, or say you’re there on purpose. You can change your mind.

◷

Privacy by design

Your data stays on your phone

Kharu keeps your activity and learning in private storage on your phone. There is no account, advertising, or analytics SDK. An optional beta summary and crash reports are explained in the privacy policy.

Why the Android permissions?

Usage access
See which chosen app is on screen and for how long. Kharu never reads screen content.
Display over other apps
Show a check-in, pause, or quiet-hour screen over a chosen app.
Notifications & foreground service
Keep check-ins timely, with an ongoing notification and Stop action, plus optional reminders.
Battery settings
Some phones stop background check-ins without an exemption.
Internet
Send only the optional beta summary and crash reports.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Does Kharu read my messages?

No. Usage access shows which app is in front and timing. It does not read messages or anything on screen.

Does it block apps?

Ordinary check-ins are choices you can answer or dismiss. Hard mode is an optional pause screen you choose for an app.

Why does it need these permissions?

Usage access counts visits, display-over-apps shows timely cards, and the foreground service keeps them running while a chosen app is open. See the full explanation.

Why do some phones need battery settings?

Some phones, including some Samsung models, stop background services. Setting Kharu to Unrestricted can help check-ins arrive on time.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Kharu is available for Android only.
