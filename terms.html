Simple terms for using Kharu during the Android beta.

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Kiran Sabne

Using Kharu

Kharu is an Android app that offers check-ins and tools for apps you choose. It is free, with no ads or in-app purchases. During the beta, you must be at least 18 years old.

Your choices

You choose which apps Kharu watches, which permissions to grant, and whether to use optional features such as Hard mode and beta sharing. You can stop check-ins and delete data in the app. Android settings and phone behavior may affect whether a check-in appears on time.

Health and safety

Kharu is a self-reflection tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. You remain responsible for your own decisions and device use.

Availability

The beta may change or have interruptions. Kharu is provided “as is” to the extent allowed by applicable law. We do not promise that every check-in will appear or that any particular outcome will follow.

Privacy and contact
