Join the beta

Try Kharu on your Android phone before it launches, and help shape what it becomes.

Kharu’s beta is a Google Play closed test for adults 18 or older on Android 9 or later. It is free, has no ads and needs no Kharu account.

How to join

Use the same Google account in all three steps: the one signed in to Google Play on your phone.

  1. Join the testers group. Open groups.google.com/g/kharu-app-testing and choose Join group.
  2. Accept the test on Google Play. Open play.google.com/apps/testing/app.kharu and choose Become a tester.
  3. Install Kharu. Open Kharu on Google Play and install. If Play says the app is not found, wait a few minutes after step 2 and try again.
1. Join the group →2. Become a tester3. Install

New versions arrive through Google Play. Kharu also shows a small “update” card when Play has one ready; your data stays.

What we ask of testers

  • Pick the apps you want Kharu to watch, then use your phone as usual for two to three weeks.
  • Answer the check-ins honestly, including “not now”. Tap “Helps” or “Annoying” when Kharu asks.
  • Answer the short questions on days 3, 7, 14 and 21. Each is one tap, and you can skip.
  • Tell us what confuses or annoys you: You → Send feedback, or email kiransabne04@gmail.com.

What is shared, and what never is

Kharu works on your phone. Sharing with the beta is optional, off until you choose it, and you can turn it off any time in You → Share with the beta.

Daily summary · automatic, if you choose

Once a day, counts in broad groups under a random tester code: total time per kind of app (never per app), how many check-ins were due, shown, skipped or held back and what you tapped, whether you stayed away 15 minutes, how many hours monitoring worked, setup steps, how often Kharu interrupted you, Play update steps, crash reports, phone maker and Kharu version.

Never sent

App names, what is on your screen or in any app, messages, anything you type (including your why), exact times, contacts, photos, location, your name or Google account. Nothing is sent for advertising, and nothing is sold.

Learning file · you send it. An optional, more detailed file (one row per check-in moment, apps shown as “feed app 1”) that you can review and send yourself. It never leaves the phone on its own.

Full details: privacy policy.

How it helps

  • Better timing. We learn which check-ins help people step away and which arrive at the wrong moment, so fewer, better-timed check-ins.
  • Less annoyance. “Annoying” taps, switch-offs and skipped check-ins show where Kharu asks too much.
  • Things that work on every phone. Monitoring hours and crash reports show where a phone maker stops Kharu in the background, so we can fix it.
  • Easier setup. Setup step counts show where people get stuck.

About 1 in 10 eligible visits gets no check-in on purpose, so we can tell whether check-ins really help. Beta data is not used to train AI models; that would need a separate, new agreement.

Leaving and deleting

  • Turn off sharing in You → Share with the beta at any time; nothing more is sent.
  • Delete what was already sent in You → Share with the beta → Delete what was sent. Sent records are also deleted 90 days after they arrive.
  • Leave the test from the Play testing page and the group, or simply uninstall. Uninstalling removes everything Kharu kept on your phone. See data deletion.
