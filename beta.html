Join the beta

How to join Kharu’s Android closed test and share optional feedback.

Kharu’s beta is for adults 18 or older using Android. Places are offered through Google Play testing.

How to join

Email kiransabne04@gmail.com to ask for a closed-test invitation. If a place is available, use the Play opt-in link we send with the Google account on your Android phone, then install Kharu from Google Play. A public install link is not available yet.

Ask for an invitation →

What we ask testers to do

Choose apps you want Kharu to notice, use your phone as usual, and tell us which check-ins help. One-tap feedback is available in the app. You can optionally turn on “Send a daily summary” in You; it is off by default and described in the privacy policy. Feedback by email is also your choice.

How to leave

Turn off check-ins or uninstall Kharu at any time. You can leave the Play test through the opt-in link in your invitation. Uninstalling removes on-device data; if you enabled the beta summary, use your tester code to request deletion of sent records.

Questions? kiransabne04@gmail.com.
