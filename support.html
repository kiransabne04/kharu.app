Support

Help with Kharu check-ins, permissions, and feedback.

Need help? Email kiransabne04@gmail.com. In Kharu, You → Send feedback opens your email app so you can write to us yourself.

Common fixes

Check-ins stopped

Check that check-ins are on. On phones that restrict background work, open Android battery settings for Kharu and choose Unrestricted. Make sure the ongoing notification is present.

A card is not showing

Check that Display over other apps is allowed for Kharu and that the app is one you chose. Kharu also stays quiet on some visits during the beta.

No activity data

Check that Usage access is allowed for Kharu in Android settings. It may take time to learn your usual visits.

Want to reset or leave?

Open You to reset learning or delete on-device data. See the deletion guide for sent beta records.

Sending feedback

Use You → Send feedback in the app, or email us directly. If you choose to attach diagnostics, review the file first. Please avoid sending private screen content or messages.
