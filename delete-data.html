Delete your data

How to remove Kharu data from your phone and request deletion of beta records.

There is no Kharu account to delete. You can remove on-device data in the app or by uninstalling it. Sent beta records need a separate request.

Delete data on your phone

  1. Open Kharu and go to You → Delete all data.
  2. Read the confirmation and choose Delete.

This clears dated usage and check-in history, learned patterns, chosen apps, settings, your why, and pending crash reports. Undated acorn totals, spending, and nest items stay on the phone so the nest remains. You can also use You to delete only activity history or reset learning.

To remove all on-device app data, including the nest, uninstall Kharu. This does not delete beta records already sent.

Request deletion of sent beta records

  1. Find your tester code in You → Share with the beta.
  2. Email kiransabne04@gmail.com with the subject “Kharu beta data deletion” and include the tester code. You do not need to provide your app history.
  3. We will respond within 30 days and delete beta summaries and crash reports tied to that code.

If you have already uninstalled Kharu and lost the tester code, email us so we can explain what can be identified. Sent records are linked to the code, not a Kharu account or your email.

What remains

Delete all data in the app keeps only undated nest totals and items on that phone. Uninstalling removes them too. A beta-record request removes records associated with your tester code from the beta database; it does not change data on your phone or copies you chose to share elsewhere through Android’s share sheet. Email correspondence about your request is handled separately.

Questions? kiransabne04@gmail.com.
